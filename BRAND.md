# OURLASH BRAND GUIDELINES

## Brand Position

OurLash is a premium lash studio specializing in natural-looking lash treatments. Every experience should feel calm, professional, and trustworthy.

---

## Brand Personality

- Elegant
- Minimalist
- Professional
- Warm
- Detail-Oriented
- Trustworthy

---

## Brand Voice

Write in a calm, confident, and friendly tone.

Avoid exaggerated marketing language.

Focus on clarity, comfort, and professionalism.

---

## Core Values

- Enhance natural beauty
- Quality over quantity
- Attention to detail
- Hygiene & safety
- Personalized experience
- Honest communication

---

## Design Principles

- Mobile-first
- Clean layout
- Spacious white space
- Premium minimalism
- Soft and elegant animations
- Every element must have a purpose

---

## Photography Style

- Real client results
- Natural lighting
- Minimal editing
- Neutral background
- Focus on eyes and lashes
- Authentic over perfect

---

## Color Palette

Primary
- Navy

Secondary
- White

Accent
- Soft Beige

Use color sparingly to maintain a premium appearance.

---

## Typography

Elegant.

Easy to read.

High contrast.

Never use decorative fonts for body text.

---

## User Experience

Visitors should feel:

1. Calm
2. Trust
3. Comfortable
4. Interested
5. Confident to book

---

## Motion

Animations should be:

- Smooth
- Subtle
- Fast
- Never distracting

---

## Do

✓ Keep the interface clean.

✓ Prioritize readability.

✓ Use generous spacing.

✓ Maintain visual consistency.

✓ Focus on the client experience.

---

## Don't

✗ Don't overcrowd the layout.

✗ Don't use flashy animations.

✗ Don't overuse colors.

✗ Don't use aggressive sales language.

✗ Don't sacrifice usability for aesthetics.

---

## Brand Goal

Create an online experience that feels as professional, comfortable, and thoughtful as the treatment itself.
