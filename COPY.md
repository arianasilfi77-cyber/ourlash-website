# FAQ

## Section Label

Frequently Asked Questions

---

## Heading

Pertanyaan yang Sering Ditanyakan

---

## Introduction

Berikut beberapa pertanyaan yang paling sering kami terima sebelum appointment.

Jika Anda masih memiliki pertanyaan lain, jangan ragu untuk menghubungi kami melalui WhatsApp.

---

### Apakah Lash Lift aman?

Ya.

Treatment dilakukan menggunakan produk profesional dengan prosedur yang tepat dan disesuaikan dengan kondisi bulu mata setiap pelanggan.

---

### Berapa lama hasil Lash Lift bertahan?

Rata-rata hasil dapat bertahan sekitar 6–8 minggu, tergantung pada siklus pertumbuhan bulu mata alami dan cara perawatannya.

---

### Apakah treatment ini terasa sakit?

Tidak.

Sebagian besar pelanggan justru merasa rileks selama proses treatment berlangsung.

---

### Apakah saya perlu datang tanpa makeup?

Ya, kami menyarankan Anda datang tanpa makeup pada area mata agar proses treatment dapat dimulai dengan lebih optimal.

Apabila masih menggunakan makeup, area mata akan dibersihkan terlebih dahulu sebelum treatment dimulai.

—-

### Apakah saya masih boleh menggunakan mascara setelah Lash Lift?

Boleh.

Namun kami menyarankan menunggu setidaknya 24 jam setelah treatment agar hasil dapat terbentuk dengan optimal.

---

### Apakah saya boleh menggunakan softlens saat treatment?

Demi kenyamanan selama proses treatment, kami menyarankan Anda melepas softlens terlebih dahulu sebelum appointment dimulai.

Anda dapat memasangnya kembali setelah treatment selesai.

—-

### Siapa yang sebaiknya tidak melakukan Lash Lift?

Treatment mungkin tidak disarankan bagi Anda yang sedang mengalami infeksi pada area mata, memiliki iritasi, atau kondisi tertentu yang memerlukan penanganan medis terlebih dahulu.

Jika ragu, silakan konsultasikan kepada kami sebelum melakukan booking.

---

### Berapa lama proses treatment?

Lash Lift membutuhkan waktu sekitar 60 menit.

Lash Lift + Tint membutuhkan waktu sekitar 70 menit.

---

### Apakah tersedia Home Service?

Ya.

Kami melayani home service untuk area tertentu di Yogyakarta.

Silakan lihat bagian Area Layanan untuk informasi mengenai cakupan wilayah dan biaya perjalanan.

---

### Bagaimana cara melakukan booking?

Booking dilakukan melalui WhatsApp.

Cukup tekan tombol "Booking Sekarang", isi informasi yang dibutuhkan, lalu kami akan membantu mengonfirmasi jadwal appointment Anda.

---

### Bagaimana jika saya harus menjadwalkan ulang appointment?
Silakan hubungi kami melalui WhatsApp sesegera mungkin, setidaknya 24 jam sebelum jadwal treatment.
Kami akan dengan senang hati membantu menyesuaikan jadwal sesuai ketersediaan appointment.
---

### Apakah saya akan mendapatkan panduan aftercare?

Tentu.

Setelah treatment selesai, Anda akan mendapatkan panduan aftercare yang dapat diakses kapan saja melalui QR Code maupun website OurLash.

### Is Lash Lift safe?

Yes.

Our treatments are performed using professional products and techniques, carefully adapted to the condition of your natural lashes.

---

### How long do the results last?

Results typically last between 6–8 weeks, depending on your natural lash growth cycle and how well you follow the aftercare instructions.

---

### Is the treatment painful?

Not at all.

Most clients find the experience relaxing and comfortable throughout the appointment.

---

### Should I arrive without eye makeup?

Yes.

We recommend arriving with clean eyes whenever possible so the treatment can begin smoothly.

If you're wearing eye makeup, we'll gently cleanse the area before starting.

---

### Can I wear mascara after a Lash Lift?

Yes.

However, we recommend waiting at least 24 hours after your appointment to allow the lift to fully set.

---

### Can I wear contact lenses during the treatment?

For your comfort, we recommend removing your contact lenses before the appointment begins.

You may wear them again once the treatment has been completed.

---

### Who should avoid getting a Lash Lift?

A Lash Lift may not be suitable if you currently have an eye infection, irritation, or another condition affecting the eye area.

If you're unsure, please contact us before booking and we'll be happy to advise you.

---

### How long does the treatment take?

Lash Lift

Approximately 60 minutes.

Lash Lift + Tint

Approximately 70 minutes.

Yes.

### Can I reschedule my appointment?

### Will I receive aftercare instructions?

Absolutely.

# FIRST 24 HOURS

## Heading

The First 24 Hours Matter

---

Please avoid the following during the first 24 hours after your treatment:

• Getting your lashes wet.

• Steam, saunas, or excessive heat.

• Rubbing or pulling your lashes.

• Applying mascara.

• Sleeping in a way that puts pressure on your lashes.

---

# AFTER 24 HOURS

## Heading

Daily Lash Care

---

To keep your lashes looking their best:

• Clean the eye area gently.

• Avoid oil-based products around the lashes whenever possible.

• Brush your lashes gently using a clean spoolie.

• Avoid rubbing your eyes.

• Handle your lashes with care to help maintain longer-lasting results.
